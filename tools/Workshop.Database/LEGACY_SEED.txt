Legacy fictional dataset
========================
Command (after building the project in Release):
dotnet tools/Workshop.Database/bin/Release/net10.0-windows/Workshop.Database.dll --seed-legacy --apply

Without --apply, the utility only prints targets and current counts.

Targets: local DB_MasterCrm and the legacy application connection, restricted to
DB_MasterCrm or db80735. Platform Master and Tenant A are not seeded.

Adds 200 Customers, Inquiries, JobOrders, JobTasks, Payments, Complaints and
Feedbacks to EACH distinct target, preserving existing records. Also adds five
fictional workshop staff profiles (not login accounts). Totals may exceed 200.

All records are fictional. Names and vehicle models are plausible; email uses
the reserved .example domain and phone numbers contain XX to avoid contacting
real people. No email or SMS is sent.

Each batch has 50 jobs in each of the four lifecycle stages. Queued and active
jobs have a 50% deposit, while completed/released jobs are paid in full. Every
job has one service task and a converted inquiry. Completed jobs have two
feedback responses each. Twenty low ratings generate urgent complaints; the
other 180 complaints cover reception, communication and quotation issues.

The tool uses the normal EF Core domain rules, including staff eligibility,
deposit checks, inspection confirmation, payment validation and audit logging.
Database identities and estimate/receipt numbering are generated normally.

Each database batch is atomic, protected by a transaction-owned application
lock. A completion audit marker prevents duplicate insertion on reruns or
ambiguous commit retries. The two databases commit independently.

Before committing, the tool checks the 200-per-module counts, completed task
states and released payment balances, then repeats verification after commit.
Pending migrations or unexpected database names stop execution.
