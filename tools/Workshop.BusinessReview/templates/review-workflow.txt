PROPOSED HISTORICAL REVIEW WORKFLOW — INSPECTION ONLY

Available now: static read-only HTML/JSON report. Authentication, assignment, evidence
upload and approval persistence described below are proposals, not deployed features.

Roles
- Authorized service advisor: inspect customer/job history; locate original records;
  document customer enquiries. Cannot change historical financial assertions.
- Workshop manager: corroborate service/job identity and original work documentation.
- Finance reviewer: assess authoritative totals, receipt history and discrepancies.
- Business owner/designated approver: approve evidence standards, reporting policy
  and separately scoped correction proposals. Do not self-approve financial changes.

Inspection screen/report
1. Open a job by its actual source database + JobOrderId; show customer/status and
   original reference. Show estimate link as stored, including an unlinked source
   association when applicable. Never generate a link because names/totals match.
2. Display three distinct figures: authoritative recorded job total; stored estimate
   total; existing itemized sum. Show signed difference and original receipts. Keep
   nonvoid paid, balance and credit calculated against the original job total.
3. Show separate badges for evidence class, approval provenance and arithmetic status.
   Reconstructed, verified historical, and unresolved legacy are not interchangeable.
4. Display source keys, line IDs, backfill note, original reference text, timestamp
   equality warning, and exact audit references. Mark missing evidence Unknown.
5. Offer view/print/evidence-inspection only. No reprice, approve estimate, classify
   payment, create missing transaction, relink or synchronize-finances action.

Future decision record (outside operational financial rows)
Source database; entity type/key; source snapshot hash; issue/category; source
document identifier/hash; reviewer identity; reviewer time; independently evidenced
historical approval time (nullable); evidence assessment; proposed action; reason;
independent approver; decision/version; prior decision reference. Preserve original
assertions and attach amendments instead of overwriting history.

Suggested review states
Awaiting evidence -> Evidence received -> Independently reviewed.
Possible outcomes: unresolved legacy retained; arithmetic discrepancy retained with
qualification; historical evidence verified; separately scoped correction proposed.
No state transition automatically changes prices, totals, receipts, payer identity,
estimate approval/link or job status. Reviewed does not mean historically approved.
An accepted unresolved legacy record can remain usable indefinitely.

Current register state
Every review remains Not reviewed by business; Reviewer/Decision are unassigned.
No owner, signature, approval date or historical agreement has been invented.

Controlled access
Distribute this customer-bearing package through an existing access-controlled share
to authorized staff. The static files have no login/authorization implementation.
Any future hosted review feature must enforce server-side read permissions, minimum
necessary data access, evidence retention, review audit logging and separation of
review from operational write permissions. Hosting/deployment is not authorized here.
